about

Business background, technical execution

My background is in Business Administration, complemented by intensive training in Applied AI & Data Analytics. I'm particularly interested in building solutions where data doesn't stop at a report or a model, but becomes something people can actually use to make better decisions.

Through my work at Digilians, I've worked across the full data workflow — from raw files to deployed applications. I've delivered end-to-end ML and BI pipelines across 400K+ records, working on problems ranging from sales and operational analytics to accident severity, inventory risk, financial sentiment, and smart-device usage.

What drives the work is a combination of business thinking and technical problem-solving — understanding what a stakeholder actually needs to decide, then building the pipeline, model, or dashboard that gets them there.
